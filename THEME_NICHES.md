# Theme expansion: audience-focused KDP candidates

The five additions below broaden the catalog from animal interests to hobby,
learning, and seasonal readers. These are **positioning candidates**, not claims
of proven demand, low competition, sales, or profitability. Live Amazon and
Open Library listings could not be accessed from the cloud environment's
restricted network, so current rankings and competition were not verified.

| Command theme | Reader niche and positioning | Timing | Entries |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `gardening` | Gardeners and plant lovers; a hobby-oriented gift or large-print activity book | Evergreen, with spring/gardening gift opportunities | 123 |
| `baking` | Home bakers and dessert enthusiasts; ingredients, techniques, breads, and pastries | Evergreen, with gift and holiday-baking angles | 127 |
| `travel` | Travel and geography enthusiasts; destinations, capitals, landmarks, and journey vocabulary | Evergreen | 128 |
| `space` | Stargazers and astronomy enthusiasts; planets, stars, missions, and observing vocabulary | Evergreen | 136 |
| `christmas` | Holiday puzzle readers; festive traditions, foods, decorations, music, and Nativity vocabulary | Seasonal | 138 |

All five support standard and genuine large-print editions, easy/medium/hard or
incremental difficulty, unique puzzle names, shaded theme answers, and series
history. Each bank has straightforward and harder clue styles. General English
crossing fill is included, as in the original themes.

Book titles and subtitles are original templates. The icons are original,
black-and-white vector drawings; they do not use third-party logos or stock art.
Christmas includes both secular festive traditions and Christian Nativity
vocabulary. The travel and space themes are also available under `geography`
and `astronomy` command aliases.

## Example commands

```sh
python crosswordgenerator.py gardening easy 50 --edition large-print
python crosswordgenerator.py baking medium 50
python crosswordgenerator.py travel incremental 50
python crosswordgenerator.py space hard 50
python crosswordgenerator.py christmas easy 50 --series "Christmas Puzzle Collection"
```

For a commercial listing, choose an accurate reader angle and description based
on the actual edition. For example, only the large-print edition should be
marketed with that label. Final listing keywords, pricing, and categories can be
chosen after checking current marketplace results; the generator creates the
interior, not a KDP listing or a sales forecast.
